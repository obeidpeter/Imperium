Optional listening

Playback is optional and starts only with Play. Stop or leaving this tab stops playback. This page does not record a subjective listening verdict.

What is measured

Duration, channels, sample rate, PCM size, peak amplitude, RMS, fraction at or above 0.999 amplitude, and leading silence below 0.001 amplitude. “Valid” means finite, non-silent decoded samples and a supported channel/sample-rate range. Clipping and silence are reported for review.
