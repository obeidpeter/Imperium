# Campaign 3D verification — version 0.11

Production revision **f73082fe872e**, verified **12 October 2026**. All nineteen battles and six political Contests across twelve chapters now use the shared 3D presentation, with the same legal rules, saved state and optional 2D tactical view. This report records the completed checks for this release. [Version 0.10 observations](showcase-verification.md) remain historical evidence rather than measurements of the expanded campaign.

- **339/339 tests passed**, with no failures, skips or cancellations, after legal fixture preparation. Node's runner completed in **48,455.5899 ms**. Coverage includes actor equipment and poses, finite terrain geometry, stage labels and picking, portrait camera zoom, retained-canvas lifecycle, rule/save purity, campaign access and offline dependency classification.

Its 25 complete encounter-start checkpoints and recorded action sequences are regenerated by `scripts/prepare-tests.mjs` on a fresh source checkout. They contain earned progression, not edited ranks or injected victories.
- **11/11 asset/offline checks passed**. These cover exact local asset integrity, build budgets, shell-only installation, every chapter's shared 3D dependencies, failed downloads, retry, version isolation and navigation fallback. The dedicated cold-Chapter-XI worker simulation forces a missing stage module, confirms that readiness stays false, then retries successfully and reuses cached shared URLs for Chapter XII. The real browser check below is separate from this mock-network protocol test.

The [encounter inventory](../campaign-3d-encounters.md) records all 25 actual 8 × 8 layouts, roles, terrain and branch-specific rendering needs. Automated routes demonstrate legality and completion; they do not establish enjoyable pacing or emotional impact.

## Real browser rendering and play

These checks used Chromium 154 on Windows in the desktop in-app browser. A browser viewport is not a physical phone, and scripted review is not an independent human playtest.

- The final real-WebGL harness sweep passed **25/25 scenes and 268 assertions**, with no captured console warnings or errors. It used legally reached checkpoints and production stage/controller code with the actual chapter and rank. Checks cover visible model/label correspondence, hidden-unit absence, authoritative tile anchors, optional grid, retained canvas, full-save equality, reduced-motion command output, animation cancellation and renderer disposal. All six Contests additionally checked the thirteen-person electorate and distinct stance/Resolve labels.
- A full legal **Move in the Chapter IX cavalry encounter** (`ch9-wing`) and **Senate Persuade** in `ch11-accounts` completed through the production animation timeline with motion enabled. Each rendering sample preserved the committed save exactly. These are labelled fixture-based rendering checks, not claims of manually playing those whole chapters.
- Through the normal campaign interface, a legally earned Chapter II start was imported and both marsh and rearguard encounters were played to victory on **turn 9 each**, with **zero allied withdrawals**. Both results were recorded; accepting **Immunis** opened Chapter III. This UI trace begins at the imported earned checkpoint, rather than at New Game.
- In the production Senate scene, **360 × 800** portrait had a 345 px document and 345 × 384 px canvas; **844 × 390** landscape had a 829 px document and 452 × 432 px canvas. Neither document overflowed horizontally. Portrait zoom-out was exercised; automated camera tests separately cover monotonic zoom. These observations do not establish touch ergonomics or complete visibility without vertical scrolling.
- Switching **3D → 2D → 3D** retained turn 2, support 6/13 and the speaker's position 4E. Chamber labels distinguish delegate stance from speaker Resolve; compact labels reveal names and details on hover or selection. Withdrawn speakers remain represented and retain their votes, while picking ignores their inactive figures.

The harness does not read or write normal campaign storage. Each action sample replays a legal prefix from its recorded start, commits through the rules API and checks that presentation leaves that state unchanged.

## Cold later-chapter offline check

A fresh production origin on localhost port 4369 imported the legally earned Chapter XI checkpoint directly, without walking through Chapter I. After the UI reported **Chapter XI available offline**, the serving process was stopped. Reload and Continue opened the 3D Senate scene from its cache.

With the server still stopped, the speaker moved from **2D to 4E**. The UI previewed Persuade on **Veterans delegate 2**, changing stance **+2 → +5**; confirming it and ending the turn produced **turn 2, support 6/13** and **Saved**. A second reload/Continue, still with the server stopped, retained turn 2 and position 4E. The server was subsequently restarted. The retained UI snapshot is `offline-senate-ui.txt` in the review evidence. No console errors or warnings were captured in the final production check after offline play.

This verifies a cold later-chapter cache and persistent legal play in this desktop browser on localhost. It does not test a system-wide network toggle, browser storage eviction, an operating-system crash, a physical installed PWA or long-term offline durability. Chapter readiness covers that chapter, not the explicit full-campaign download or separate study page.

Each Balanced-quality profile sampled **120 actual rendered frames after 18 warm-up frames**, at **1440 × 1100 CSS viewport**, device ratio 1, and a **1368 × 600 CSS canvas**. These were idle legally reached encounters without an active action animation. All samples preserved the complete save. CPU rendering/submission time, intervals between actual stage renders and browser RAF intervals were recorded separately.

| Chapter XI Senate | 13.8 / 18.0 | 18.5 / 44.9 | 16.7 / 33.3 |

A separate 120-frame cavalry run at Performance quality submitted **281,964 triangles**, compared with Balanced's **467,052**. Its CPU p50/p95 was **22.4/45.4 ms**, stage intervals **32.7/71.2 ms** and RAF intervals **16.7/49.9 ms**. The runs experienced different browser/host load; these timings do not establish a speed ranking between quality modes. They verify the lower geometry workload, not a measured frame-rate improvement.

These are one desktop browser's recorded samples. They are not GPU timings, a guaranteed frame rate, a before/after speed comparison or evidence of phone performance. In particular, the cavalry p95 and irregular frame intervals leave further performance work to assess on target devices.

## Remaining limits

The characters and scenery are stylized original code-native models. They use scripted poses, shared base faces with stable minor variations and simplified reconstructed equipment; they are not scanned characters or captured performances. Horse hoof placement assumes one ground plane per actor. No exact-uniform or photorealism claim is made.

Physical-device installation and touch play, screen-reader review, audible mixing, long sessions, independent human enjoyment and the 90–150 minute pacing target remain unverified. The all-scene sweep covers the recorded legal route; alternative campaign choices remain covered by rule tests rather than a complete visual inspection of every branch. This release has completed the checks above, not those broader evaluations.
