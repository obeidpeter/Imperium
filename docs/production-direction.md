# Version 0.8: playable presentation

The user's current request is to make every necessary improvement to the graphics and gameplay after finding the previous result basic and dated. This iteration prioritises the playable scene, readable interactions and authored movement over additional website ornament.

## Work in this iteration

- Make the battlefield or chamber the dominant screen, with a compact mission bar, company roster and action dock. Keep detailed rules and named selectors accessible in disclosures.
- Point at a figure's body to select it. Point at reachable ground to preview movement, or an opponent to preview an available attack. A second deliberate activation of that same target commits it; Confirm remains available. No timing-sensitive double-click is required.
- Preserve exact rule previews. Automatic intent selection cannot spend resources or bypass legality. Out-of-range figures remain inspectable.
- Add authored character action frames where validated, with truthful existing directional fallbacks for uncovered roles and facings. Do not label procedural bending as newly authored animation.
- Combine locally bundled ground materials and natural props with continuous ground, restrained tactical markings and atmospheric scene lighting. Interaction highlights and the optional grid retain spatial clarity.
- Preserve campaign progression, deterministic outcomes, save compatibility, political costs, offline readiness reporting, reduced motion and non-spatial controls.

## Asset and performance tradeoff

The old 2,000,000-byte limit for the entire campaign prevented useful expansion of the art. Version 0.8 retains a 2,000,000-byte compressed initial shell budget, but permits up to 8,000,000 for chapter I and 24,000,000 for the full campaign. These are maximum build budgets, not expected download sizes. Chapter artwork stays lazy-loaded. The decoded image cache is bounded to 40 entries and 64 MiB; performance mode and reduced motion remain available. Actual build sizes are reported in the verification document.

## Acceptance evidence

Review the real opening encounter, a later field battle and a political chamber. Compare like-for-like scenes to the preserved version 0.7 source. Check visible animation frames, pointer and keyboard commands, previews before spending an action, small-screen layout and enlarged text. Run the rule and save suites, legal full-campaign autoplay, production build and asset checks. Record what was actually inspected and distinguish browser emulation from physical-device testing.

This is a 2D isometric game with authored raster art and deterministic tactics. It does not claim a 3D engine, motion capture, universal animation coverage, or validated human enjoyment.
