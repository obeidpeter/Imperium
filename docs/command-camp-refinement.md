# Command, camp and character refinement

This pass starts from local commit `9b41441` on `codex/camp-character-followup`. That commit preserves the preceding tested camp/character work. This document covers the subsequent implementation; it is not a deployment or release sign-off.

## Player-facing changes

- **Review orders** appears while a squad battle is paused. Each friendly squad shows its current or pending order, destination/target, formation, reserved Command and arrival delay. Replace opens that squad's existing action list. Cancel removes unapplied orders; active behavior continues. Shared formation reservations transfer to the remaining squads, and earlier-round points cannot be refunded. Hold uses its ordinary cost and delay.
- **Optional errands** show the next action derived from actual items, flags and dialogue stages. All 26 ordinary errands and nine company reports have guidance. Track pins one chosen task above camp; Go uses the normal walking and interaction commands. Collected/dropped items, recovering or absent companions, completion, chapter changes and reload are handled. Tracking is a local UI preference outside campaign saves.
- **Battle debriefs** show up to three distinct moments from the retained battle log, with recorded times and available positions. Retry advice refers to observed events or the actual mission failure. Sparse older saves receive an explicit fallback; this is not a reconstructed complete replay or a claim that an incidental event caused defeat.
- **Camps** have connected service wear, shaded supply/household bays and purpose-based exterior prop courts. Interior wear follows traversable cells; exterior aprons must meet an open boundary across their width. Buildings and full prop footprints remain separated. All twelve camps gain service paths and shade; the eleven regional layouts group their existing accessories by service while Chapter V retains its authored fortress composition. Workers have distinct work/rest beats, a writing stylus where appropriate, and attend the approaching player without moving their interaction anchors.
- **Characters** receive local Blender mesh/weight refinements and shared animation adjustments. Per-file provenance and pose/anchor evidence accompany the art pipeline. No Tripo generation or additional paid assets are used. The original painted faces still limit extreme close-up quality; geometry refinement does not replace their source textures.
- **Performance evidence** now separates repeated settled-frame observations, turning-camera measurements, software rendering and interrupted checks. Hiding the page interrupts the check through a visibility event, including a hide/show between frames. Quality and adaptive-resolution settings are restored. The Windows-only check uses the default browser graphics backend, records the actual renderer and verifies exported-state preservation across sampling and resume.

## Boundaries

No new gameplay RNG, encounter version or saved campaign field is introduced. Invalid commands preserve state. Scenery and actor poses do not alter navigation, targeting, collision, damage timing or campaign progress. Existing offline and rendering ceilings remain in force.

Physical phones, first-time players, a timed human campaign and subjective headphone/speaker review remain pending. The player-test kit specifies the observations needed before changing pacing or claiming those checks complete. Automated campaign completion is not evidence of enjoyment or human completion time.
