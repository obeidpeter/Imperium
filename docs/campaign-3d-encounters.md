## Political positioning and state

`CHAMBER_SCENARIOS` supplies nine distinct delegate coordinates per chamber. The protagonist begins at (1,3), ally at (1,5), rivals at (7,3) and (5,7), using zero-based coordinates. Delegates occupy real rule cells and block movement; furniture must not invent additional obstacles. Voters have `hp: 1` only as a rule implementation detail: labels should show **stance, bloc and interest**, not a combat health bar. Speakers use **Resolve**. A speaker at zero Resolve withdraws but remains in the thirteen-person electorate. Hidden tactical figures must remain absent from models, labels and picking until revealed.

The three blocs are Veterans, Landholders and Civic. Interests vary by issue: service/security/law, security/grain/law, law/grain/security, service/law/grain, law/office/service and security/law/grain. Persuade, Promise, Threaten, Record, Address, Rebut and Denounce are arguments, not weapon attacks. The normal command API and exact target coordinates remain authoritative.

## Source and fixture lineage

The inventory uses `src/data/campaign.js`, `src/data/encounters.js`, `src/core/battle.js`, `src/core/contest.js`, and a replay of `tests/fixtures/legal-route-trace.json` from New Game, seed 275. `scripts/campaign-3d-fixtures.mjs` creates complete start checkpoints and legal action pairs for browser inspection; its generated file is excluded from source packages and regenerated during test preparation. Fixture-based browser rendering is labelled separately from playing through the complete UI. It neither writes campaign saves nor claims independent human or physical-device validation.
