# Version 0.15 presentation assets

This update adds one generated character-material atlas and fourteen generated audio files. The [asset manifest](asset-manifest.json) records local export sizes and SHA-256 digests. The [generation record](graphics-prompts/presentation-v015.json) contains the actual submitted prompts and selected model/voice metadata. It deliberately excludes provider response objects and temporary signed media URLs.

The character atlas was generated from text through OpenAI’s built-in image-generation tool, with no input image. The requested 2048 × 2048 image was returned at **1254 × 1254**. Its four equal 627-pixel quadrants depict linen, leather, iron mail and subtle skin texture. The preserved PNG master is `work/v015/character-materials-master.png` in the task workspace, outside the public source package. The local runtime export uses Pillow WebP quality 84, method 6, without resizing or creative repainting. It is **438,826 bytes**. Runtime code samples each quadrant to a shared 256-pixel colour map and neutralizes its average colour so character tint remains controlled; procedural bump, roughness and failure fallbacks remain. This is a surface-texture upgrade to the project’s original procedural characters, not an imported sculpt or a motion-captured model.

The audio was generated through the connected Runway tool with no reference recording. Three instrumental beds used `lyria-3-clip`; four ambience beds and three isolated foley clips used the tool’s sound-effects workflow; four fictional-character lines used `eleven_v3` with the named provider voices recorded in the manifest. Voice lines use original project text. These are generated performances, not recordings of the fictional characters’ historical counterparts. Delivered MP3s were bundled without re-encoding or audio edits and totalled **3,273,036 bytes**. Version 0.29 re-encodes the three music beds (LAME VBR `-q:a 6`) to reduce download size, keeping the decoded sample count identical; the bundled audio now totals **2,234,206 bytes**, and `asset-manifest.json` records each original's size and SHA-256. Prompt descriptions such as “loop-friendly” describe generation intent; they are not a claim of sample-perfect loops or a professional audio review.

All new files are local and deferred from the initial installation. Shared character materials enter every chapter’s 3D resource group. Chapter audio groups include exploration music and common foley; battle chapters add battle music and regional ambience. Chapter II uses rain, Chapters V, VI and IX use dry ambience, and other battle chapters use frontier ambience. Chapters VII, VIII, X, XI and XII additionally include council music, ambience and the council voice. Arrival and Varro lines are in Chapter I; the rally line is available from Chapter IV. The explicit complete offline download includes all fourteen audio files. Audio never passes through the image-decoding cache.

Asset records and hashes establish provenance, not exclusive copyright, non-infringement or a license grant. Provider terms and the repository’s license notices govern their respective scope; this document does not assert ownership of provider technology or third-party works. No signed URL, media CDN, runtime generation service or extra downloaded library is required to play the bundled assets.

The new encounter precincts, gates, service courts, landing approaches and civic backdrops are original geometry assembled from the existing local material library. Their layout metadata is presentation-only. New structures stay outside the complete playable rectangle; authored terrain, movement, objectives, river boundaries and picking remain controlled by the original game state.
