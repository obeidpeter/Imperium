# A chronicle shaped by service

The campaign still has twelve chapters and twenty-five encounters. Military command, governorship, admission to the Senate, and the imperial succession remain distinct achievements. The new scenes and company roll connect those achievements to the people and decisions that brought you there.

## Read the moment you have reached

Arrival introduces a chapter’s people and problem. Choosing your course brings a response to that choice and a briefing for the next encounter. When you return, the scene describes the result just committed and the next undertaking, instead of repeating the chapter’s arrival.

The first oath follows completed training. The lead seal is received when you swear, and the same original oath returns at accession. Later promotions include the chapter’s field record: which formations returned, which withdrew, and which veterans earned commendations.

Your reading position is saved separately for each scene. Moving to a new scene starts its first passage; restoring your chronicle returns to the saved passage in the current scene. Reading and previews do not spend a turn or alter the random state.

The existing long-term callbacks remain in the journal and epilogue. Immediate responses now accompany all twelve chapter decisions as well. The rescue of Lucan, the withdrawal involving Varro, and the priority at the Danube crossing also change the people or columns that need protection on the field. The named characters’ permanent fates still follow clearly stated decisions, rather than an uncontrolled allied turn.

## The company that comes with you

The service roll follows Lucan—or Sabinus after the chosen loss of Lucan—alongside Aulus Naso, Marcus Felix, Titus Firmus, and Gnaeus Rufus. Early missions deploy only part of this company; greater command brings more of them into the field. Each record has service, commendations, and recovery.

| Record | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Service | One entry for each committed field deployment. A rescued company member can also receive service for that extraction. |
| Commendations | A deployment earns a commendation when every allied formation returns. Three commendations add one maximum Health; six also reduce incoming morale loss by one. Commendations are capped at twelve. |
| Recovery | A withdrawn veteran receives two recovery steps. A returning veteran at one-third Health or below receives one. Each completed encounter reduces existing recovery by one step; a fresh injury can renew it. |
| Deployment while recovering | Each remaining recovery step reduces starting Health by one, with a floor of eight. Recovery never removes the veteran or seals a chapter. |

Service and commendations are recorded only when you commit the victory. Retry restores the company and reading checkpoint along with the rest of the encounter-start state. Reopening a won encounter and committing it cannot award the same service twice. Defecting opponents do not inherit the records of company members who were never deployed.

If Lucan is left in the marsh, Sabinus takes his place with a fresh personal service record. Lucan’s earlier story remains in the chronicle; his honours are not quietly reassigned to another person.

## A province with visible consequences

The governor still allocates exactly six convoys, with at least one for the river garrisons, farming villages, and town granaries. The recorded allocation now describes what those recipients can bring to the following challenges.

| Decision | Effect in the provincial challenges |
| --- | --- |
| River convoys beyond the first | Five additional starting morale per extra convoy for allied formations on the grain road. |
| Farm convoys beyond the first | Five additional integrity per extra convoy for the guarded grain stores. |
| Three or more town convoys | Landholder delegates begin the governor’s tribunal one stance point more supportive. |
| Shared tenure | Civic delegates begin the tribunal one stance point more supportive. Both communities retain a place in the settlement. |
| Settler priority | Veteran delegates begin the tribunal one stance point more supportive; Landholders begin one point less supportive. Established tenants retain their grievance. |

The relief totals and entered judgment remain part of your record after leaving office. The result scene names the people who received the grain and the judgment that now has witnesses. The established effects on Army Loyalty, Political Backing, Public Confidence, supplies, relationships, and obligations still apply.

## Continuing an existing chronicle

Version-one saves remain accepted. Saves created before these additions receive an empty reading record and a company roll with no invented past commendations or injuries. Existing chapters, choices, appointments, resources, and transaction checkpoints are retained.

An older active battle or Contest keeps its original encounter rules and layout through completion or retry. After that encounter, newly entered missions use the revised authored fields and chambers. Regression fixtures generated with the saved pre-upgrade rules check the original start, a mid-encounter save, the original deterministic turn response, exact retry, and a reload at the commit boundary.

The new story and company tests check scene order, individual choice responses, absent-character replacements, reading restoration, company transactions, recovery, and save compatibility. They do not establish first-time enjoyment, physical-device usability, or the 90–150 minute pacing target; those still need human playtesting.
